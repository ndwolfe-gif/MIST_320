
# Build Me Up Construction
## Executive Project Status Report

**Project:** Field Operations & Change Management Portal  
**Course:** MIST 320  
**Reporting Period:** End of Sprint 2

### Executive Summary

Build Me Up Construction's Field Operations & Change Management Portal is demonstrating favorable project performance following the completion of Sprint 2. The project is designed to address communication breakdowns between construction-site personnel and central project management by centralizing field reporting, safety documentation, blueprint modifications, and change-order approvals.

The project has completed all committed work across its first two sprints, delivering 78 of 108 total story points. Current Earned Value Management (EVM) results indicate that the project is performing ahead of schedule and under budget. With one sprint remaining, the team is positioned to complete the remaining development work within its established budget, provided that current performance trends continue.

### Overall Project Health

Overall project health is assessed as **GREEN**, based on current sprint execution and EVM performance. Both completed sprints achieved 100% of their committed story points. The project has an SPI of 1.08, indicating favorable schedule performance, and a CPI of 1.08, indicating favorable cost efficiency.

The projected Estimate at Completion (EAC) is $276,923 against an approved budget of $300,000. This represents a projected budget underrun of approximately $23,077. These forecasts are based on the assignment's assumed actual cost and should not be interpreted as verified financial expenditures.

### Milestone Progress and Schedule Performance

Sprint 1 delivered 47 story points across nine issues, while Sprint 2 delivered 31 points across eight issues. Although velocity decreased by approximately 34% between the two sprints, the team maintained a 100% completion rate for committed work. The reduction reflects a smaller Sprint 2 commitment and smaller average issue sizes rather than an inability to complete planned work.

The team has completed approximately 72% of the project's total estimated story points. Sprint 3 contains 30 remaining planned points, which is below the team's historical average velocity of 39 points per sprint. This provides a reasonable basis for expecting the team to complete the remaining work within the final sprint.

### Financial Performance and Budget Variances

The project's current EVM indicators demonstrate favorable financial and schedule performance. Earned Value is approximately $216,667, compared with a Planned Value of $200,000 and an assumed Actual Cost of $200,000. This produces favorable schedule and cost variances of approximately $16,667 each.

The projected total project cost of $276,923 suggests that the project can finish approximately 7.7% below its original budget. An additional $76,923 is forecast to complete the remaining work.

### Major Risks and Business Exposure

Despite positive performance indicators, several operational risks require continued attention. Unreliable job-site connectivity could interrupt field reporting, while employee resistance to adopting the new platform could limit its effectiveness. Blueprint synchronization failures and inaccurate change-order calculations could also create construction errors, rework, and unexpected expenditures.

These risks are particularly important because the project's intended business value depends on reliable communication and accurate information sharing between field personnel and central management.

### Strategic Recommendations and Next Steps

Management should prioritize successful completion of Sprint 3 while maintaining the project's current cost and schedule performance. Testing and quality assurance should confirm that core portal functionality operates reliably before final delivery.

Additional attention should be directed toward employee adoption, data security, connectivity safeguards, and accurate financial tracking of approved change orders. Deferred enhancements should remain outside the current implementation scope to prevent unnecessary cost increases and schedule disruptions.

### Executive Conclusion

Build Me Up Construction's Field Operations & Change Management Portal is demonstrating strong execution through the conclusion of Sprint 2. The team has consistently delivered its committed work, and current EVM forecasts indicate favorable schedule and budget performance.

With 30 story points remaining and one sprint left, the project appears well positioned for successful completion. Maintaining scope discipline, addressing operational risks, and validating system functionality will be essential to delivering the intended improvements in construction project visibility, financial accountability, and operational efficiency.
