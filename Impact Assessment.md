Build Me Up Field Management Portal Requested by: Regional Safety & Risk Director

The portal would connect to workers' smart wearables. If a worker without an active
crane radius, the system would sound an on site siren, lock out the machinery, and log an OSHA noncompliance report. 

SCOPE IMPACT
Build Me Up is a portal for project communication and information. This request directly conflicts with our Scope 
Statment.

- "Physical construction management or control of job-site equipment" and purchasing field hardware are both excluded. 
This was printed out in section 6 

- The portal "does not replace... physical construction equipment." printed in section 12.

Meeting the request would require new deliverables:

- Enforcing Wearables 
- Positioning software
- Geo-fence management
- Siren and crane lockout controls
- Automated OSHA reporting

SCHEDULE IMPACT:
	
The change would add many months to the estimated completion date. Even with the system renting cranes is alot more
affordable option and we would need permission from the crane owners to implement the software. Which would also conflict with 
our constraint to stay within the approved schedule found in section 11.

REASOURCE IMPACT:

This addition seems to be an unnessasry use of resources. There would be thousands of purchased hardware and some software engineering 
which can be seen in further detail below. 

- Wearables for each worker
- lockout software on cranes
- On site alarms
- Beacons for each site

RISK IMPACT:

-Connectivity is already a major risk as stated in the scope. Our updates need internet and can not be completed offline. A saftey system 
that fails when disconnected is unsafe.

- Mid lift lockouts could pose a huge risk aswell if the crane operator is interuppted it could cause the crane to shutdown which could result
in bigger problems.

-False alarms would most likely go off frequently becasue of a the new system which would lead to the laborers ignoring real alarms that go off.

-The software would be a huge liability if it were to have control over these heavy machines or osha records.

The request falls outside the approved scope, schedule, and budget. Its goal of keeping uncertified workers out of crane zones is still valid and 
supports the Safety Personnel stakeholder group.

ASSESSMENT RESULT : DEFER
