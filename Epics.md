# Build Me Up Construction
## Epic & Issue Backlog

The project will be organized into five major epics. Each epic represents a major functional area of the Field Operations & Change Management Portal.

---

## Epic: Budget & Cost Management

**Label:** `epic:budget`

Manage project budgets, expenses, cost tracking, and budget variance.

**Primary Users:**
- Project Managers
- Central Office Staff
- Administrators

**Issues:**
- Project Budget Setup
- Expense Tracking
- Budget vs. Actual Reporting
- Cost Category Management

---

## Epic: Safety Reports

**Label:** `epic:safety`

Digitize the creation, tracking, and management of job-site safety reports.

**Primary Users:**
- Field Engineers
- Project Managers
- Administrators

**Issues:**
- Submit Safety Report
- Attach Safety Documentation
- Safety Report Status Tracking
- Safety Report Search

---

## Epic: Site Checklist & Blueprint Details

**Label:** `epic:site-documentation`

Provide field personnel with centralized access to site checklists, blueprints, and project documentation.

**Primary Users:**
- Field Engineers
- Project Managers

**Issues:**
- Complete Site Checklist
- Review Checklist Progress
- Upload Blueprint
- Blueprint Version Tracking

---

## Epic: Change Order Management

**Label:** `epic:change-orders`

Manage the submission, review, approval, and financial impact of project change orders.

**Primary Users:**
- Field Engineers
- Project Managers
- Administrators

**Issues:**
- Submit Change Order
- Review Change Order
- Approve or Reject Change Order
- Track Change Order Cost Impact

---

## Epic: Users & Notifications

**Label:** `epic:users-notifications`

Manage user accounts, permissions, roles, and project notifications.

**Primary Users:**
- Administrators
- Project Managers
- Field Engineers

**Issues:**
- Create User Account
- Assign User Roles
- Manage Permissions
- Configure Notifications
